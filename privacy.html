Privacy Policy

Application Information

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Galaxy Star Wallpaper (package name: com.galaxy.star.wallpaper.wall), provided as a free application.

Information Collection and Use

Galaxy Star Wallpape is designed with privacy in mind. The application does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

Any settings or imported reference images remain locally on your device and are used only to support the drawing experience.

Camera Permission

The application requires access to your device’s camera solely to display a real-time augmented reality overlay while you draw.

Camera images are not recorded, stored, analyzed, or shared by the application.

Third-Party Services

Galaxy Star Wallpape does not integrate analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking services. No user behavior data is shared with external providers.

Children’s Privacy

Galaxy Star Wallpape does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since no personal data is collected, the app is safe to use for general creative learning purposes.
